WHO ARE WE?

WE ARE TEAM NOX FRC 11702 & FTC 35307. THIS TEAM, FOUNDED ON THE IDEOLOGY THAT INNOVATION NOT ONLY DEVELOPS BUT THRIVES IN AN INCLUSIVE ENVIRONMENT, STRIVES TO FOSTER A SUPPORTIVE SPACE FOR STUDENTS WHO HAVE HAD NO PRIOR EXPERIENCE IN FIRST, OR HAVE BEEN EXPOSED, BUT HAVE FELT UNDERVALUED, UNDERREPRESENTED, OR UNEMPOWERED WITHIN THEIR COMMUNITY.
