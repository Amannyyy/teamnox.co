WHERE IT BEGAN

OUR MISSION

Team Nox was initiated by a group of friends who simply wanted to do robotics together. Founded on the belief that innovation not only develops but thrives in an inclusive environment, we strive to create a supportive space for students who are new to FIRST or who have felt undervalued, underrepresented, or unempowered within their communities.

Our mission is to build a safe, student-led, and welcoming community where every student has the opportunity to explore STEM, grow their skills, and develop a genuine sense of belonging.

WHERE WE'RE GOING

OUR VISION

Our vision is to create a lasting impact on the STEM community that offers each student an equal opportunity to explore their ambition in robotics, gain confidence in their abilities, and excel as not only engineers and leaders, but as role models for the next generation of innovators.

Team Nox operates on three key principles: Ignite, Innovate, and Inspire, striving to become a shining star within the New York City STEM community. Through mentorship, outreach, and accessible FIRST programs, we aim to inspire future generations of innovators who continue to learn, compete, lead, and give back.
