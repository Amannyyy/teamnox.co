FRC 11702

ADVANCED

Nox FRC 11702 is our advanced, large-scale robotics program for high school students ready to take on complex engineering challenges. Members work in specialized divisions such as mechanical, design, electrical, software, strategy, and operations to design and build a full-size competition robot for the FIRST Robotics Competition.

The FRC program gives students the opportunity to work on larger and more complex systems while learning how different engineering disciplines come together. Members develop experience with CAD, fabrication, programming, electrical systems, autonomous controls, strategy, and project management.

FRC 11702 is designed to give students an experience similar to working on a real engineering team, where individual specialties work together to create one reliable and competitive system.

FTC 35707

FOUNDATIONAL

Nox FTC 35707 is our hands-on robotics program focused on helping students build a strong foundation in engineering and competitive robotics. Members work closely together to design, build, wire, program, test, and operate a smaller competition robot for the FIRST Tech Challenge.

The FTC program emphasizes experimenting with new ideas, rapid prototyping, and developing skills across multiple areas of robotics. Students can explore different roles, learn Java programming, build mechanisms, develop autonomous routines, and contribute directly to multiple parts of the robot.

FTC 35707 helps students discover which areas of robotics interest them most while developing technical skills, confidence, teamwork, and experience for more advanced engineering projects.
