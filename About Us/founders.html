AMAN

CO-FOUNDER

Aman is a senior at Aviation High School, where he studies aircraft maintenance while working toward his Airframe and Powerplant certification.

During freshman year, his interest in engineering led him to join his school's robotics team and later additional teams to expand his experience. Through these opportunities, Aman has developed skills in CAD, Java programming, mechanical design, and robotics.

His goal with Nox is to compete at the highest level while creating an environment where students of all experience levels can have fun, learn the fundamentals of STEM, and grow alongside their teammates. He hopes to inspire the next generation of innovators by sharing his knowledge, experience, and passion for engineering.

SAFA

CO-FOUNDER

Safa is a junior at The Young Women's Leadership School of Astoria, where her interest in STEM and computer science led her to join her school's robotics team.

Through FIRST, she discovered the importance of building a community while developing skills in assembly, block coding, and awards. She later became Software Lead and expanded her programming knowledge through Java and Android Studio.

Having learned so much through robotics, Safa's goal with Nox is to share her knowledge while creating a welcoming environment where students can learn, grow, and explore STEM. She hopes to inspire the next generation of innovators by teaching others and helping them develop the same passion for robotics and technology.

LUCJAN

CO-FOUNDER

Lucjan is a senior and part of his school's second graduating class. His school was founded by NYCFIRST and helped propel him to achieve great things both in and outside of FIRST.

He was introduced to FTC during his freshman year through his school's robotics team and later joined a community team. During his junior year, he co-founded Nox after developing his mechanical, teamwork, and leadership abilities.

Through Nox, Lucjan hopes to address challenges within his community and create a serious yet enjoyable pathway into STEM for students of all backgrounds. He hopes to leave knowing that he helped build a team with a lasting impact on the future of STEM.
